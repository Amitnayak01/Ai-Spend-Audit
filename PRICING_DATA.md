## Common Optimization Patterns Found

1. **Cursor + Copilot overlap** — Teams using both save avg. $19-40/seat by picking one
2. **ChatGPT Team vs multiple Plus** — 4+ individual Plus ($80/mo) > Team ($100/mo) only marginally
3. **Enterprise overkill** — Teams <25 rarely need Enterprise features
4. **API waste** — Avg. 30% reduction achievable through caching and model-tier switching
