# Go-To-Market Strategy — SpendScan AI

## Target Segments

**Primary:** Engineering managers and CTOs at Series A-B startups (20-200 employees) adopting AI tools faster than their procurement process can keep up with.

**Secondary:** CFOs/finance teams bringing AI tool costs under control as they scale.

**Tertiary:** Freelancers managing 3+ AI subscriptions.

## Acquisition Channels

### 1. Organic / SEO
Target keywords:
- "AI tool cost comparison" (1.2K/mo, low competition)
- "GitHub Copilot vs Cursor cost" (800/mo)
- "ChatGPT team plan vs individual" (600/mo)
- "AI subscription management" (400/mo)

Content: Blog posts comparing every tool pair we support. Each links to the audit.

### 2. Product Hunt Launch
Target #1 Product of the Day in "Productivity" + "Finance". Launch Tuesday. Pre-warm 200+ upvotes.

### 3. Developer Communities
- Hacker News "Show HN"
- Reddit: r/MachineLearning, r/startups, r/devops
- Twitter/X: Anonymized "we found X% waste" stories

### 4. Cold Outreach
Use audit data to identify high-waste industries. Target CTOs with personalized industry benchmarks.

## Conversion Funnel

```
Landing page → Start Audit (15% CTR)
    → Step 1 complete (85%)
    → Step 2 complete (70%)
    → Email submitted (60%)
    → Email opened (45%)
    → Report shared (8%)
```

## Monetization Roadmap

**Phase 1 (Free):** Audit tool as lead gen. Build email list.
**Phase 2 ($99/mo):** Monthly AI spend monitoring with alerts.
**Phase 3 ($499/mo):** Team dashboard with budget tracking.
**Phase 4 (Custom):** Enterprise consulting + contract negotiation.
